Day 01 — Beginning My DevOps Engineering Journey

Date: November 11, 2026
Programme: DevOps Engineering
Training Institution: TS Academy
Cohort: Aurora Cohort

1. Overview

Today marked the beginning of my DevOps Engineering training with TS Academy as part of the Aurora Cohort.

Our first class was introductory. It provided an opportunity to begin familiarising ourselves with the programme, its expectations, and the learning journey ahead.

For me, this is more than enrolling in a technical course. It is an opportunity to develop practical engineering skills, strengthen my understanding of modern IT infrastructure, and build the foundation for a career in DevOps and DevSecOps engineering.

2. Learning Objectives

Throughout this programme, I look forward to developing practical knowledge and skills in areas including:

* DevOps culture and principles.
* Linux administration and command-line operations.
* Networking and Bash scripting.
* Git and version control.
* Docker and containerisation.
* AWS cloud computing.
* Terraform and Infrastructure as Code.
* Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD).
* Kubernetes and container orchestration.
* Monitoring and logging.
* Secure infrastructure and software delivery practices.

These are programme learning objectives, not a list of topics covered in today’s introductory class.

3. My Approach to Learning

I intend to approach this programme with consistency, curiosity, and a practical mindset.

My learning approach will include:

1. Attending classes and documenting key lessons.
2. Practising technical concepts in my own lab environment.
3. Completing assignments and practical exercises.
4. Recording challenges and how I resolve them.
5. Building projects that demonstrate my understanding.
6. Sharing genuine learning experiences with the technology community.

4. Connection to My DevSecOps Journey

Alongside this formal training, I maintain an independent DevSecOps engineering learning journey.

I see a valuable connection between DevOps and security: modern engineering should not only focus on building and deploying systems efficiently but also on making those systems reliable and secure.

I intend to keep both learning tracks properly documented while allowing the knowledge gained from each to complement the other.

5. Reflection

The most important lesson from today is that every meaningful technical journey begins with a willingness to learn and the discipline to remain consistent.

I am looking forward to the practical sessions, technical challenges, projects, and opportunities to grow throughout this programme.

This is Day 1. There is a lot to learn, and I am ready for the work ahead.

Learning. Building. Automating. Securing.
