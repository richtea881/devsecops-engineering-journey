# DevSecOps Engineering Journey

Welcome to my technical learning and professional development portfolio.

The objective is to develop the knowledge and practical skills required to build, automate, secure, monitor, and maintain modern technology infrastructure.

I am an IT professional expanding my technical capabilities through structured training, independent study, practical laboratories, and project-based learning.

My development follows two complementary learning tracks.
